About Upgrade Timer Calc

Upgrade Timer Calc is an independent tool that turns potion boosts into exact upgrade completion times. Enter how much time is left, add the potions you plan to use, and it tells you precisely when the upgrade will finish — then optionally sets an alarm for that moment.

Built and maintained by @ajvillam0r.

What it does

  • Builder, Research and Pet potion math with configurable multipliers
  • Plain day / hour / minute date calculator
  • A floating overlay mode so the calculator stays on top of your game
  • One-tap alarms for the computed completion time
